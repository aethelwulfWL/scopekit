A TWO-MINUTE START

From brief to proposal.

  1. Choose your starting point. Templates contain example deliverables and fees. Customize them for the actual project.
  2. Make the boundaries explicit. Add client responsibilities, exclusions and revision limits.
  3. Save your work. Browser autosave is convenient; “Back up draft” is your portable copy.
  4. Send a clean proposal. Export HTML or print to PDF. Review the document and agree final terms with your client.

No account. No uploads. No tracking. The app doesn’t send proposals, collect payments or record signatures.
